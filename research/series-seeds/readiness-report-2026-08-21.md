## Series-level claim and authority audit

The sequence consistently treats trust registries, transparency services, decentralized notification, scholarly identifiers, content-addressed assertions, and publish–review–curate systems as established guiding prior art. The candidate MCRP contribution remains a proposed integration around exact scientific releases, prospective claim contracts, scoped review attestations, dependency-sensitive freshness, and an event-driven automation loop.

The exact attestation binding is preserved where consequential:

`(claim version, acceptance-contract version, evidence-set digest, release digest, role, decision)`

Automated observations, agent reports, and policy projections remain distinct from scientific dispositions. Qualified humans retain authority for current MCRP scientific decisions. The drafts do not claim that MCRP has demonstrated interoperability, web-scale operation, convergence, governance legitimacy, capture resistance, adoption, improved review outcomes, scientific truth, or journal replacement.

The updated logical-claims audit adds explicit entries for claim/derivation separation, freshness, scholarly review metadata, trust-node role separation, faceted reviewer standing, disagreement preservation, the Sybil warning, and proposed evaluation thresholds.
