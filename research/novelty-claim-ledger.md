# MCRP Novelty Claim Ledger

Status: bounded novelty gate. This ledger records what may and may not be claimed before manuscript drafting.

## Decision vocabulary

- **NO-GO:** contradicted by close prior art; do not claim as novel.
- **CONDITIONAL:** potentially defensible only if the stated evidence is produced.
- **SUPPORTING:** useful motivation or implementation choice, not itself a novelty claim.

## Required novelty package

The conditional gate remains closed until all five artifacts exist:

1. **Normative protocol:** typed entities/relations, claim versions, states, transition guards, roles, revocation, and dependency invalidation.
2. **Interoperability profile:** explicit mappings to W3C PROV, RO-Crate/Workflow Run RO-Crate, and nanopublication or Micropublication claim records.
3. **External validator:** transition checks execute outside the research-generating agent and fail closed on missing, mismatched, mutated, or stale evidence.
4. **Reference implementation and fixtures:** successful, failed, superseded, revoked, adversarial, and interruption/resumption cases with content hashes and reproducible runs.
5. **Comparative evaluation:** at minimum prompt-only, provenance-log-only, and MCRP conditions; independent human adjudication; preregistered reliability and review-effort outcomes.

## Wording guardrails

Allowed if supported:

> We introduce and evaluate a prospective protocol that binds each versioned scientific claim to preregistered acceptance criteria, immutable execution evidence, externally enforced transitions, and scoped revocable attestations, with interoperable export to established provenance and research-object standards.

Disallowed:

> We introduce the first claim-evidence-provenance graph for agentic science.

> We introduce the first system for reproducible agent research or human/agent scientific sign-off.

> Cryptographic signatures or complete provenance prove that a scientific claim is correct.
