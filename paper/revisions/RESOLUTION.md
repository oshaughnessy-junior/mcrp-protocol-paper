# Integrated review resolution

The main manuscript and runnable model supersede proposed insertion blocks where they differ. The specialist and red-team reports remain as an audit trail, including findings subsequently fixed.

- External admission and authority are explicitly in S1.
- Every S1/S2 predicate is non-exemptible. S3 exceptions are limited to predeclared platform comparisons or individual sensitivity subcases; overall sensitivity, independent challenge and exercised freshness remain required.
- Level zero is disclosure/pending, never accepted scientific conformance.
- Whole-release labels require current, undisputed, full-scope coverage.
- Graph guarantees apply only to declared propagating edges; known hidden-dependency failures remain visible.
- The carry-forward rule includes explicit currentness, assessment time/policy, known expiry/revocation and pending/disputed state; material-claim inventory edits become dirty roots.
- Historical counts are withdrawn as evidence. Separate public-source rerun (20 tests) and new semantics suite (16 tests) are labeled independently.
- Standalone PDF links point to version-tagged inventory, raw audit metadata and model source. Git version identity is explicitly not an archival DOI or preservation guarantee.

The team collaborated under one author-directed orchestration. These resolutions and tests do not establish external scientific review, real-world effectiveness, calibrated assurance, authenticated control, or archival conformance.
